## 2. Springing the Archetypal System Traps in Scientific LLMs

1. **Rule Beating / Verification Gaming**:
   - *Trap*: Agents find superficial ways to satisfy tests (e.g. passing unverified values through trivial math `x * 1.0` or vague rationales).
   - *Leverage Point*: **DREAMS Anti-Laundering Gates** and **Rosetta Dual Verification** that check semantic causality and mathematical necessity rather than superficial tokens.
2. **Drift to Low Performance**:
   - *Trap*: Agents quietly relax convergence thresholds (e.g. accepting $10^{-3}$ eV or coarse k-meshes) to avoid calculation failures.
   - *Leverage Point*: Strict **TRITONDFT Pareto Accuracy Tiers** and immutable **DREAMS Reports** that reject uncharacterized compromises.
3. **Escalation / Spinning Loops**:
   - *Trap*: Agents looping infinitely with tiny random parameter perturbations when a calculation is fundamentally intractable.
   - *Leverage Point*: The **Lee & Rondinelli 8-Attempt Recovery Budget** and **8-Rule Decision Ladder** that force a structured halt and escalation.

## 3. Top Leverage Points for Scientific Autonomous Systems
- **Leverage Point 6 (Information Flows)**: Transparent, machine-auditable provenance across all subagents via DREAMS Canvas and Graphify.
- **Leverage Point 5 (Rules of the System)**: Scientific Constitution prohibiting circularity, Ponytail VASP protocol prohibiting redundancy.
- **Leverage Point 4 (Self-Organization)**: The power of the agent to evolve and add to its own system structure via lifelong skill crystallization (`save_to_skill`).
- **Leverage Point 3 (Goal of the System)**: Aligning the system goal with rigorous, reproducible, first-principles truth rather than task-completion speed or output volume.
